About

I'm a security engineer on the FedRAMP team at Genesys, protecting a FedRAMP-authorized cloud environment on AWS. I own incident response from the moment an alert fires through containment, recovery and follow-up, and I work with development and infrastructure teams to build security into their pipelines before release.

I came to security after years in manufacturing planning and process management at Cleveland-Cliffs and ArcelorMittal. That background still shapes how I work: clear process, decisions written down, and security that makes operations stronger instead of slower.

I've served in the Air Force since 2011, first as an air transportation specialist and now as a Cyber Effects Officer in the Air National Guard, where I also help run mentorship for the airmen in my unit. On my own time I keep building: an AWS lab for offense and defense, Infrastructure as Code, and tools that connect Claude to real data through the Model Context Protocol.
