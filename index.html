I protect cloud systems built to federal standards. From the first alert to the final fix.

On the FedRAMP security team at Genesys, I own incident response for a FedRAMP-authorized cloud environment, from the first alert through containment, recovery and follow-up. I build and tune detections, tighten IAM across AWS accounts, and work with engineering teams so every release ships secure and compliant.

I didn't start in security, and that's an advantage. After a BBA in logistics management from Ohio State, I spent more than seven years at the same Cleveland steel plant, from production planning to leading a slab yard team of 112. Operations taught me that most failures are process failures, so I bring that thinking to security: find where a process breaks, then make it hold.

I've served in the Air Force for 15 years, 12 of them enlisted, and now as a commissioned Cyber Effects Officer in the Air National Guard. What ties it together is people: I'm passionate about mentorship and developing the people around me. Next, I plan to pursue an MBA with an emphasis in cybersecurity, working toward a bigger goal of helping businesses of every size use technology safely and responsibly.
